    Where to simulate

    On app.alpaca.markets switch to the Paper account, generate an API key and enter it below. The key is stored only on this computer (data/secrets, readable only by your user), is never shown on the web page, and is never shown to agents. Helm connects only to the paper environment; live keys won't work.

    On start it immediately places orders in the Alpaca paper account to reach the target holdings, then rebalances daily. It trades only this simulation's positions: other holdings in the account are left alone and not counted in strategy capital (starting cash = account equity − the market value of those holdings). No orders are placed on a day when the data update failed or is stale. One Alpaca account can follow only one simulation at a time.
